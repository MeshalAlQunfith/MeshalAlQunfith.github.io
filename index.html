02 / Findings

The average doesn't tell the whole story

The gap between average and median resolution time is the clearest starting point for further analysis.

Key observation

Some requests take far longer than the typical case.

Average closure time is approximately 111.2 hours, while the median is just 22.1 hours. That difference indicates a long tail of slower requests, but doesn't establish the cause.

620 requests (6.25%) exceeded seven days.

03 / Recommendations

What to investigate next

These are proposed next steps, not confirmed causes of delay.

Review long-running cases

Investigate requests taking more than seven days to identify recurring patterns.

Compare service groups

Examine closure-time differences by category and agency before proposing targeted changes.

Track both mean and median

Use typical and extreme closure times together to avoid misleading performance summaries.
